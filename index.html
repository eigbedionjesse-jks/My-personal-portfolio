About Me

My name is Eigbedion Jesse Oseghale. I am a Christian born in Lagos, Nigeria, from Ketu, and my family roots are in Edo State.

I have a deep love for the things of God and a strong desire to live a life that serves God's purpose and impacts people positively. I attended Majidun Senior Grammar School and I am planning to further my education.

Shortly after secondary school, I lost my father. During that period of my life, I learnt the trade of tiling, which became an important part of my journey and is currently one of the ways I work and serve people.

My story is one of growth, resilience, learning, faith, creativity, service, and purpose.
